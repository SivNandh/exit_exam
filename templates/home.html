ABOUT THE PROJECT

From behavior
to a useful signal.

Modewise uses a trained machine-learning model to estimate a person's preferred shopping mode from survey details. Inputs include shopping frequency, preferred platforms, spending habits, payment methods, and responses to ads and reviews.

Research use: Predictions are estimates from survey data, not confirmed purchases or causal marketing effects. Avoid using them as the sole basis for customer decisions.
